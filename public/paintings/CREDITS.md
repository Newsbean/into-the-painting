# The paintings on the gallery wall

These five files are photographs of the real paintings. They hang on the wall of the
gallery (`src/worlds/gallery/index.js`); nothing inside a painting's world uses them.

All five works are in the public domain, and Wikimedia Commons lists each of these
reproductions as public domain too (checked on 21 November 2026). Each file here is the
1920-pixel version served by Commons, made smaller (1400 pixels on its long side, JPEG).
